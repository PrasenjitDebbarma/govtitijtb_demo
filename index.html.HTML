From the Principal's Desk

Nurturing Technical Excellence, Discipline & Industry Readiness

Welcome to Government Industrial Training Institute, Jatanbari. Established in 1979 under the Directorate of Industries & Commerce, Government of Tripura, our institution has been dedicated to transforming aspiring youth of Gomati District and across Tripura into skilled, self-reliant technicians and entrepreneurs.

Under the Craftsmen Training Scheme (CTS) aligned with NCVT and SCVT standards, we place strong emphasis on practical, hands-on workshop training, safety practices, digital literacy, and employability skills. Our curriculum is continuously updated to bridge the gap between classroom theory and evolving industrial requirements.

I encourage all prospective trainees to make the fullest use of our dedicated faculty, equipped laboratories, and apprenticeship placement support to build a rewarding technical career.

Sri George Hrangkhawl
Principal, Govt. ITI Jatanbari (Code: GR16000003)

Online Registration & Choice Filling

Visit itiadmission.tripura.gov.in, register with mobile/Aadhaar, upload scanned documents, and select Govt. ITI Jatanbari along with preferred trades.

2

Physical Document Verification

Report to the nearest Government ITI verification center (including Govt. ITI Jatanbari) with all original certificates and photocopies within the stipulated schedule.

3

Merit List Publication & Seat Allotment

Common Merit List is generated based on qualifying exam marks (8th/10th) and state reservation norms (ST/SC/OBC/PWD/Ex-Servicemen/Women).

4

Final Reporting & Caution Money Deposit

Download allotment letter and report to Govt. ITI Jatanbari with ₹1,000 refundable caution money and Medical Fitness Certificate to confirm admission.
